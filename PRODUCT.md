# Product

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

The official music website for F.A.T.E. (Fight Against the Enemy) and Buried In Ruin. Visitors can discover each project, hear featured music, explore releases and lyrics, and open streaming services.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The existing Next.js application serves F.A.T.E. at `/` and Buried In Ruin at `/buried-in-ruin`.
- Evolve & Elevate's public artist catalogs own release titles, artwork, audio, dates, lyrics, and feature scheduling. Website presentation must preserve those live data sources and exact song slugs.
- Preview playback is user-initiated and coordinated across the website so only one player plays at a time.
- Artist logos, the F.A.T.E. portrait, and published release art are existing assets.

## Brand Commitments

The user approved artist-led F.A.T.E. and release-led Buried In Ruin heroes, using the cleaner futuristic direction from the November 2, 2026 review: precise studio styling for F.A.T.E. and restrained industrial styling for Buried In Ruin. Preserve the recognizable artist marks and project identities.
